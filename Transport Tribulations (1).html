Transport
Tribulations

In 2022, Marshalls USA conducted a study on safety in public places. It found that up to 40% of people avoided transport hubs at night, and “waiting for public transport” was considered the second most risky activity to do in public by respondents.

Public transport within the US has a notorious reputation; whether it’s through social media or the news, we often hear about bad things happening on trains, buses, or even at stations waiting for a ride. We grew up hearing from our parents to beware in these spaces, and our friends share with us their elaborate strategies to stay safe. We may even have had personal experiences that prove these concerns to be valid. So we conducted an investigation.
